  - I am loosely following this tutorial called ["Learn WebGPU"](https://eliemichel.github.io/LearnWebGPU/)
  by Élie Michel and contributors. This is
  super useful for SDL boilerplate (and a 
  very simple extension I need).
  - From several sites, I've heard that WGPU 
  is better than OpenGL,
  but it still provides a wrapper to OpenGL 3.0/3.3
  (depending on the platform).
  That should make this game pretty portable!
  - My intention is not to test on *every* version
  of every OS, because that would be crazy.
    - SDL [sets minimum versions for the
    platforms](https://wiki.libsdl.org/SDL3/README-platforms) I care about.
    - Extra platforms supported by SDL
    are great, but I don't
    want to spread myself too thin
  - I really liked the idea of the
  `wgpu` crate in rust and was
  going to follow a tutorial there.
     - What would be great is if
     I could compile parts of `wgpu`
     *and then* call them in C.
     - On the other hand, that
     may lead to a bunch of FFI
     issues (memory leaks!).
     There may be some alternatives
     (the static analyzer I want to 
     develop would work).

## Questions

- How much of WGPU do I want to use?
  - For very simple levels (e.g.,
  basic hidden object), basic images
  suffice.
  - Particle effects and cool shaders
  would be great to add. But this
  will be more involved.

- From last time: what should Manager
ABI be (just for app entry)?
  - Do I unify everything under `App`,
  or use separate `structs`?
  - The former is easier to work
  with.
  - The latter requires wrappers
  but guarantees I *only* change
  those structs.
  - Another alternative: tagged unions
    - More verbose and emulates
    the members in `App`.
